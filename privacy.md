<h1>Privacy Policy</h1>
<p>Last updated: May 2026</p>
<p>TeraQuist runs a deliberately simple, static website. We don't track you, and we collect only the information you choose to send us. This policy explains exactly what that means, and your rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR.</p>

<h2>Who We Are (Data Controller)</h2>
<p>TeraQuist, a venture incubated by Joshua8.AI with offices in McLean, VA and Rehoboth Beach, DE, USA, is the controller of the limited personal data described below. Contact: <a href="mailto:jim@joshua8.ai">jim@joshua8.ai</a>.</p>

<h2>What We Collect</h2>
<p>This site is a single static page. It has no contact forms, no newsletter signup, no cookies, and no analytics or tracking scripts. We use your device's built-in system fonts and load no third-party fonts or scripts, so simply viewing the site does not send your data to advertising, analytics, or content-delivery third parties. We do not build profiles of visitors.</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Information you email us.</strong> If you choose to email us (at <a href="mailto:jim@joshua8.ai">jim@joshua8.ai</a>), we receive whatever you choose to include — typically your name, email address, company, and message.</li>
  <li><strong>Standard server logs.</strong> Our host, Cloudflare Pages, processes routine request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site. This is handled by Cloudflare as our processor under its own terms.</li>
</ul>

<h2>How We Use It and Our Legal Basis</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>To read and respond to messages you send us</strong> — legal basis: our legitimate interests in handling enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or steps toward a contract where relevant (Art. 6(1)(b)).</li>
  <li><strong>To keep the site available and secure</strong> (server logs, via our host) — legal basis: our legitimate interests in operating and protecting the site (Art. 6(1)(f)).</li>
</ul>
<p>We do not sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising or automated decision-making.</p>

<h2>Sharing</h2>
<p>We don't share the information you send us, except with the service providers that help us operate (our website host, Cloudflare, and our email provider), with our parent company Joshua8.AI where needed to respond to your enquiry, or where required by law.</p>

<h2>International Transfers</h2>
<p>We are based in the United States, and our host and email provider process data in the US. If you contact us or visit from the EU, UK, or elsewhere, your data may be transferred to the US. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses offered by those providers).</p>

<h2>How Long We Keep It</h2>
<p>We keep email correspondence only as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any resulting relationship, then delete it. Server logs are retained for a short period by our host for security and operational purposes.</p>

<h2>Your Rights</h2>
<p>Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data; to restrict or object to processing; and to withdraw consent. Because we only hold what you've emailed us, we can act on these quickly — email <a href="mailto:jim@joshua8.ai">jim@joshua8.ai</a> and we'll take care of it.</p>
<p>If you are in the EU or UK and believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.</p>

<h2>Changes</h2>
<p>If we add features that collect data — for example, a contact form or analytics — we'll update this policy before or when we do.</p>

<p>Questions? Email <a href="mailto:jim@joshua8.ai">jim@joshua8.ai</a>.</p>
